# Ground rules

Copy to `GROUND-RULES.md` at setup. This is the durable anchor for people and agents, not a status page. The named human owner approves changes; agents propose them and explain why. Correct an error or adapt when the project changes, but do not silently rewrite the rules to fit a result. Record the change and date here or in version history. Higher-priority user instructions still apply; flag conflicts rather than guessing.

- Project purpose and stable success criteria:
- Human owner of these rules, and decision authority/boundaries:
- Verified external rules, deadline and source links (mark unknowns):
- Hard constraints: privacy, data access, compute, budget, submissions:
- Ways of working that matter for this project (including agent authority and escalation):

Do not put the leading candidate, next experiment, checkpoint, or evidence for the next decision here; those belong in `CURRENT.md`.

Example (fictional): “Choose the most accurate method that finishes within five minutes; only the human owner can authorize an external submission.” This remains in force as candidates change. If the time limit changes, the owner updates this anchor and notes why.
